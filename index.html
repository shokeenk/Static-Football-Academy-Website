About Us

About Static Football Academy

Founded in 2024 at Surajmal Football Ground, Static Football Academy started with a simple belief: consistency builds great players.
Through daily hard work and the dedication of founder and coach Tajish Shokeen, Static has grown with a focus on developing technically strong, disciplined, and confident footballers through structured coaching, teamwork, fitness, and real match experience.

Mission

Develop footballers with discipline, confidence and technical excellence.

Vision

Become one of Delhi's leading youth football academies.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups do you train?

We train three age groups: U10, U14 and U18 — each with its own curriculum and weekly schedule.

Where is the academy located?

We train at Maharaja Surajmal Stadium, West Delhi. Exact ground address is shared once you book a trial.

How do I register?

Fill out the contact form below or WhatsApp us directly — we'll confirm a free trial slot for your player's age group.

Can beginners join?

Yes — the U10 program is built for players just starting out, focused on fundamentals and enjoying the game.

Do you offer trial sessions?

Yes, every new player gets a free trial session before enrolling.
